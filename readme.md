# FitPlan — Your Fitness, Your Way

FitPlan is a responsive fitness-planning website that helps users explore workout routines and food ideas based on their personal fitness goals, experience level, weekly schedule, and workout location.

## ✨ Features

- **Personalized Starter Plans:** Generate fitness suggestions based on your selected goal.
- **Multiple Fitness Goals:** Choose fat loss, muscle gain, or general fitness.
- **Flexible Workout Schedule:** Select between 2 and 6 workout days per week.
- **Experience-Based Planning:** Choose beginner, intermediate, or advanced.
- **Home and Gym Options:** Get workout suggestions based on your preferred location.
- **Food Suggestions:** Explore food ideas to complement your fitness routine.
- **Responsive Design:** Clean, modern interface designed for different screen sizes.
- **Interactive Interface:** Generate a plan, edit your preferences, or start over.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- HTML5 — website structure
- CSS3 — styling and responsive layout
- JavaScript — interactive features and plan generation

## 🚀 How to Run

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open the project folder.
3. Open `index.html` in your web browser.
4. Select your fitness goal, experience level, workout days, and workout location.
5. Click **Generate My Plan** to view your starter plan.

No backend or database setup is required.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of FitPlan is to provide a simple and accessible starting point for people who want to build healthier habits through structured workout suggestions and practical food ideas.

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

FitPlan provides general fitness suggestions for informational purposes only. It does not replace professional medical advice or individualized guidance from qualified fitness and nutrition professionals.
